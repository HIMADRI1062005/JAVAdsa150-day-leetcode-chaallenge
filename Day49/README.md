# 🚀 Day 49 - Total Waviness of Numbers in Range I

Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Total Waviness of Numbers in Range I

## ✅ What I did

Calculated the total waviness of all numbers within a given range.

## 💡 Approach

Analyzed the pattern of digits for each number and computed its waviness value.

For every number in the given range:

- Evaluated its digit sequence
- Calculated the contribution to waviness
- Added it to the final answer

The key was identifying the pattern correctly and implementing it efficiently.

## 📚 What I learned

- Pattern observation techniques
- Digit-based problem solving
- Mathematical reasoning
- Careful implementation of conditions
