## 🧩 Problem

### 1344. Angle Between Hands of a Clock

🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/angle-between-hands-of-a-clock/

## ✅ What I did

Calculated the smaller angle formed between the hour hand and minute hand of a clock.

## 💡 Approach

Used mathematical formulas to determine the position of both clock hands.

- Minute hand moves 6 degrees per minute
- Hour hand moves 30 degrees per hour
- The hour hand also moves gradually as minutes pass

After calculating both angles:

- Found the absolute difference
- Returned the smaller angle between the two possibilities

## 📚 What I learned

- Clock angle calculations
- Geometry-based problem solving
- Mathematical modeling
- Handling fractional values accurately
