# 🚀 Day 9 - Container With Most Water

Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Container With Most Water  
🔗 https://leetcode.com/submissions/detail/1986219181/

**What I did:**  
Tried to find the maximum area of water that can be contained between two lines.

**Approach:**  
Used the two pointer technique. Started with left and right pointers and moved the smaller height inward to maximize area.

**What I learned:**  
- Two pointer technique  
- How to optimize from brute force O(n²) to O(n)  
- Thinking in terms of area and constraints
