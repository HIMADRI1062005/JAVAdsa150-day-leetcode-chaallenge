# 🚀 Day 50 - Total Waviness of Numbers in Range II

Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Total Waviness of Numbers in Range II

## ✅ What I did

Calculated the total waviness of numbers within a given range while handling larger constraints compared to the previous version of the problem.

## 💡 Approach

Used digit-based analysis and optimized counting techniques to efficiently calculate the waviness contribution of numbers in the range.

Instead of checking every number individually, the solution focuses on counting patterns and reducing unnecessary computations.

## 📚 What I learned

- Digit DP concepts
- Pattern-based counting
- Optimizing brute-force approaches
- Handling large constraints efficiently
