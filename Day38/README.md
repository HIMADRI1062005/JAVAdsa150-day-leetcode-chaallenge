Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Majority Element II  
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/majority-element-ii/

**What I did:**  
Tried to find all elements that appear more than ⌊n/3⌋ times in the array.

**Approach:**  
Used the Boyer–Moore Voting Algorithm extension.  
Maintained two candidates and their counts because there can be at most two majority elements greater than n/3.

**What I learned:**  
- Extension of Boyer–Moore Voting Algorithm  
- Candidate elimination concept  
- Optimized frequency tracking without extra space
