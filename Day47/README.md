## 🧩 Problem

### Earliest Finish Time for Land and Water Rides I

## ✅ What I did

Calculated the earliest possible finish time by choosing the best combination of land and water rides.

## 💡 Approach

Checked all possible ride combinations and calculated the total completion time for each option.

For every valid combination:

- Considered the ride start time
- Added the ride duration
- Compared the final completion times

Tracked the minimum finish time throughout the process.

## 📚 What I learned

- Simulation-based problem solving
- Handling multiple conditions carefully
- Finding optimal results from different combinations
- Time calculation logic
