Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock  
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

**What I did:**  
Tried to find the maximum profit by choosing the best day to buy and sell a stock.

**Approach:**  
Tracked the minimum price so far and calculated the profit at each step.

**What I learned:**  
- Greedy approach  
- Tracking minimum value  
- Optimizing from brute force to O(n)  

---

## 💭 My thoughts  
This problem looked simple, but it was interesting to think about how to maximize profit step by step.  

It helped me understand how keeping track of minimum values can simplify the solution.
