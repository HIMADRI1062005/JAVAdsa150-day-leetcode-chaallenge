🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/gcd-of-odd-and-even-sums/

## ✅ What I did

Solved a mathematical problem that required computing the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of the sum of odd numbers and the sum of even numbers.

## 💡 Approach

The solution is based on mathematical observations rather than brute force.

The overall idea was:

- Calculate the required odd and even sums according to the problem statement.
- Use the Euclidean Algorithm to efficiently compute the GCD of the two sums.
- Return the final GCD as the answer.

Instead of performing unnecessary calculations, the solution relies on the properties of GCD, making it both simple and efficient.
