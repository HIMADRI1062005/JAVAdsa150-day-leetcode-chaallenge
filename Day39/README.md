Today I solved one problem as part of my DSA journey.

## 🧩 Problem

### Maximum Product Subarray  
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-product-subarray/

**What I did:**  
Tried to find the contiguous subarray that has the largest product.

**Approach:**  
Tracked both the maximum and minimum product at every step because a negative number can turn a small product into a large one.

**What I learned:**  
- Dynamic tracking technique  
- Importance of handling negative numbers carefully  
- Why both max and min products are needed  

---

## 💭 My thoughts  
This problem was definitely more tricky than Maximum Subarray because multiplication with negative numbers changes everything.  

At first I only tracked the maximum product, but then I realized the minimum product is also important because multiplying two negatives can create a large positive value.  

Really interesting problem for improving problem-solving logic.
